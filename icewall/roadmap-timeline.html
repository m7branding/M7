WEB3 Q1 2027 · March

Smart Contract Goes Live

ICEWALL moves on-chain. An audited smart contract takes over custody of every fragment, claim and future trade. Chain selection — Sui or Polygon — will be finalised based on gas cost, NFT tooling, and community input.

  • Decision factorsTransaction cost, NFT standard maturity, community vote
  • AuditIndependent third-party review before mainnet deployment

CHARITY Q1 2027 · March

The Conservation Split Is Locked In

Before a single NFT is minted, the split is already written into the contract: 50% of all future trading royalties and 50% of every newly minted ICE fragment are permanently routed to the ICEWALL Conservation Fund. Not a promise — code.

  • MechanismOn-chain, unchangeable royalty + mint split
  • ReportingEvery distribution publicly viewable on-chain
08
MINT Q2 2027 · April

Mint Machine Launches

Every claimed fragment becomes eligible for exchange through the Mint Machine. Each resulting NFT is unique and self-owned — island of origin, claim date, fragment number and an optional personal inscription are written permanently into its metadata. From here, your fragment isn't just held. It's owned, displayed, traded, and carried into whatever comes next.

  • MetadataIsland, fragment #, claim date, personal note
  • OwnershipFully self-custodied, tradable on open marketplaces
  • ConversionOne-way, fragment → NFT
09
AIRDROP Q2 2027 · May

ICE Fragment Airdrop

A thank-you that can't be bought. Early adopters — the first Wall, before islands, before Web3 — receive a wave of unique ICE fragments airdropped straight to their wallets. No queue, no claim window. Just a record of having been early.

CHARITY Q2 2027 · June

First Conservation Deposit

The Mint Machine's first trades settle, and the royalty split activates for the first time. The inaugural deposit into the ICEWALL Conservation Fund is published on-chain and publicly viewable — the first of many the contract now guarantees.
